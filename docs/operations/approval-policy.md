# Approval Policy

## Auto-Allowed In MVP

- local draft generation
- internal summaries
- lead research from public sources
- report drafts
- meeting brief drafts
- task planning
- read-only CRM analysis after a connector is approved

## Requires Human Approval

- sending email
- posting social content
- contacting third parties
- changing CRM records
- changing deal stages
- exporting customer data
- creating a quote to send externally
- using paid APIs beyond a defined budget
- production deployment
- legal, tax, financial, employment, or regulatory claims

## Blocked Until Policy Exists

- fully autonomous outreach
- autonomous contract generation as final legal text
- autonomous payment or subscription changes
- autonomous deletion of customer records
- autonomous credential use outside a connector grant

## Output Statuses

Every agent output should carry one status:

- `draft`
- `needs_review`
- `approved`
- `rejected`
- `blocked`
- `verified`
- `unverified`
