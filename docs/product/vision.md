# Product Vision

Saga Agent Ops is a managed AI workforce system for Saga Teknoloji.

It starts as an internal operating layer for sales and delivery, then becomes a customer-facing managed AI worker package.

## Beachhead

The first MVP is B2B revenue and operations support:

- keep pipeline visible
- research leads
- prepare offer drafts
- create meeting briefs
- suggest follow-ups
- produce weekly business reports

## Differentiation

Saga Agent Ops should not compete as a generic chatbot or agent builder.

The differentiation is:

- Turkish and KVKK-aware operating model
- human approval for risky actions
- cost-controlled AI workers
- task ledger and audit trail
- sector playbooks
- managed delivery, not self-serve complexity first

## First Customer

Saga Teknoloji itself is the first customer. The system must prove internal value before external packaging.
